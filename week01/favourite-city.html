About My City

Lagos(my city)is Nigeria's largest city and a major port on the country's southwestern coast, with a population recently estimated between 17 and 21 million people.That makes it one of the most populous urban area in Africa and one of the fastest-growing megacities in the world.The city sits along the Lagos Lagoon, protected from the Atlantic Ocean by barrier islands and sandbars like Bar Beach.Strong transport links an international airport, three seaports, several major expressways, and a growing rail network help Lagos handle the bulk of Nigeria's exports and support one of Africa's most modern urban rail systems.

Beyond its size, my city is West Africa's leading financial and business hub, home to major banks, the Nigerian stock exchange, a growing tech scene with several billion-dollar startups, and even the world's largest planned oil refinery. It's also a cultural powerhouse: home to Nollywood (Nigeria's film industry), the Afrobeats music scene that launched stars like Wizkid, Tems, and Davido, and institutions like the National Museum and several public universities. Together, these strengths makes my city both an economic engine and a cultural centerpiece for Nigeria and the wider region.
