Lagos

Lagos is Nigeria's largest city and one of the fastest-growing megacities on the planet, home to tens of millions of people packed into a coastal stretch of southwestern Nigeria. Built around lagoons, creeks, and barrier islands that separate it from the Atlantic, the city's geography has shaped everything from its bridges to its famous "go-slow" traffic. It's the undisputed financial capital of West Africa major banks, the Nigerian stock exchange, and a growing wave of billion-dollar tech startups all call it home. Add in busy seaports, an international airport, and an expanding rail network, and Lagos handles the overwhelming bulk of Nigeria's trade with the rest of the world. But Lagos isn't just about business. It's a cultural powerhouse hosting Africa's biggest fashion week and one of the continent's largest art fairs and a city that never really slows down, blending hustle, creativity, and resilience into one relentless urban rhythm.
