Rapid Rush Expeditions

Rapid Rush Expeditions exists to bring people together on California's rivers through safe, unforgettable whitewater adventures that build lasting memories and stronger bonds. Our mission is to give every guest, from first-timers to seasoned rafters, an exhilarating and well-run river experience, led by experienced guides who truly care and backed by decades of expertise. We live by a simple creed: safety comes first and fun rides right beside it, every guest is treated like family, we protect the rivers that give us our adventures, and we make every trip easy to plan and impossible to forget. Above all, we stand by our motto: Experience the Unforgettable Together.

History

Rapid Rush Expeditions began in 2005, when founder Daniel Mercer launched his first raft on a river in the Sierra Nevada foothills and set out to share the thrill of whitewater with the public. He started small, running trips for friends and family out of his garage. Rafting was still a growing sport in the area, and Daniel saw a chance to offer adventures that were both exciting and safe. In 2009, while working as a teacher, Daniel started an outdoor adventure club for his students. It became Rapid Rush Youth Adventures, a program that still introduces young people to rivers and the outdoors. In 2012, the company held its first Guide School to train new guides as demand for trips grew. In 2015, it expanded to a second river and began offering overnight and multi-day trips. As the years went on, Rapid Rush Expeditions earned permits on several premier rivers and a reputation for professional guides, well-kept equipment, and trips for every skill level, from gentle family floats to advanced whitewater. The company stayed family-run, with Daniel's children and relatives joining the team as guides, office staff, and trip leaders. It also got through the hard year of 2020, when travel restrictions sharply reduced business, and came back stronger by putting even more focus on safety and service. Today, nearly twenty years after that first trip, Rapid Rush Expeditions welcomes thousands of guests each year, and it still follows the goal Daniel started with: to experience the unforgettable together.
