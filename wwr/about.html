Rapid Rush Expeditions

Rapid Rush Expeditions brings people together on California's rivers through safe, unforgettable whitewater adventures. Led by experienced guides, every guest is treated like family, and safety always comes first. Our motto: Experience the unforgettable together.

History

Rapid Rush Expeditions began in 2005, when founder Daniel Mercer launched his first raft in the Sierra Nevada foothills to share the thrill of whitewater with the public. Since then,the family-run company has grown from small trips for friends into a trusted outfitter with professional guides, youth programs, and adventures for every skill level. Today it welcomes thousands of guests each year, still following the goal Daniel started with: to experience the unforgettable together.
