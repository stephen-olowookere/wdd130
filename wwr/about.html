Rapid Rush Expeditions

Rapid Rush Expeditions exists to bring people together on California's rivers through safe, unforgettable whitewater adventures that build lasting memories and stronger bonds.Our mission is to give every guest, from first-timers to seasoned rafters, an exhilarating and well-run river experience, led by experienced guides who truly care and backed by decades of expertise. We live by a simple creed:safety comes first and fun rides right beside it, every guest is treated like family, we protect the rivers that give us our adventures, and we make every trip easy to plan and impossible to forget. Above all, we stand by our motto is to Experience the unforgettable together.

History

Rapid Rush Expeditions began in 2005, when founder Daniel Mercer launched his first raft in the Sierra Nevada foothills to share the thrill of whitewater with the public. Since then,the family-run company has grown from small trips for friends into a trusted outfitter with professional guides, youth programs, and adventures for every skill level. Today it welcomes thousands of guests each year, still following the goal Daniel started with: to experience the unforgettable together.
